Are you a freelance web developer in Lagos?
Yes. I'm Adeshina Quadri, a freelance web designer and full-stack developer based in Lagos, Nigeria. Through QD Designs I build business websites, online stores and web apps for clients in Lagos and across Nigeria. More about working with me in Lagos →
Is QD Designs a web design company in Nigeria?
QD Designs is my freelance web design and development studio in Lagos. You work directly with me, the designer and developer, from the first call to launch, with no middlemen or hand-offs between teams.
Do you work with clients in Ogun State and Abuja?
Yes. I work with clients across Ogun State (including KENWIN School in Ewekoro) and with clients in Abuja, fully remotely over WhatsApp, calls and video. See web developer in Ogun State and web developer for Abuja businesses.
Do you offer a free consultation?
Yes. Every project starts with a free consultation and a free quote. Tell me about your business and what you need, and I'll recommend the right kind of website. Chat with me on WhatsApp →
How much does a website cost in Nigeria?
It depends on what you need: the number of pages, whether you need an online store or logins, and integrations like WhatsApp ordering or email. I don't use one-size-fits-all packages. Instead, you get a clear, free quote after a short chat about your project.
Do you build affordable websites for small businesses?
Yes. Many of my clients are small and growing Nigerian businesses: food vendors, cake shops, schools and skincare brands. I scope each project around what your business actually needs, so you pay for what helps you sell, not for extras.
What kinds of websites do you build?
Business and brand websites, e-commerce stores, school websites, hotel and hospitality sites, real-estate platforms, marketplaces and full SaaS web apps. Recent work includes HomeLink, ContaTrack, Hive9ja, Sportellectual, Fofo Deluxe and Esteehi Hotel & Lounge.
Can customers order from my website through WhatsApp?
Yes. I build one-tap WhatsApp ordering into stores and business sites so customers can send their order straight to your WhatsApp, which is how many Nigerian customers prefer to buy.
Do you handle hosting, domains and deployment?
Yes. I can set up your custom domain, host your site on fast, reliable platforms like Vercel or GitHub Pages, and launch it for you, so you don't have to deal with the technical side.
Are you open to full-stack developer jobs and roles?
Yes. Alongside freelance projects I'm open to full-stack developer roles, remote or in Lagos. My core stack is React, Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase and Tailwind CSS. Connect on LinkedIn or download my CV.
